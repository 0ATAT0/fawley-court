# Design context — Fawley Court deal portal

Written for the frontend-design skill so a later session does not have to infer
it. This is an existing, deployed identity; the brief in August 2026 was a
structural reframe, not a restyle.

**Target audience.** One principal — the Align partner running the deal — with
occasional onward sharing to a capital partner. Not a pitch audience. He is the
operator of the deal, not the audience of an argument.

**Use cases.** He arrives with a question and wants the answer in one or two
taps: what does it return at £50m, what does Reschio charge, what does the room
say about parking, what is the GOP margin against the cohort. Read on an iPad in
a meeting, often offline, sometimes on a phone in a car.

**Tone.** Restrained, editorial, document-grade. A working instrument, not app
chrome: hairline rules, tabular figures, a navy subject highlight, generous but
purposeful white space. Harvey sophistication, Sequoia editorial calm. No
promotional adjectives anywhere — the register rule governs the copy as much as
the design.

**Constraints.**
- One self-contained HTML file. No build step, no external requests, no web
  fonts: the portal must open with a dead connection, so the house Playfair /
  Inter pairing cannot be used and the system sans stack stands in. This is a
  deliberate, standing deviation from the Strand house style.
- Offline-capable PWA, service-worker cached.
- 44px touch targets, WCAG AA contrast, no horizontal page scroll at 390px.
- Every figure verbatim from a gated source; `verify.mjs` is the design's
  hardest constraint, because a layout change that re-words a cell fails it.

**The differentiating move.** Answer first, argument on demand: every view leads
with its exhibit and folds its reasoning behind a rule, and prose never sits
alone in half a canvas.
